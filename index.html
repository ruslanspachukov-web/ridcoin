RIDCOIN (RID)

An in-game community currency on the Polygon PoS Chain.

About

RIDCOIN (RID) is an ERC-20 token on the Polygon PoS Chain. It is used as an in-game currency within a community game, for example to receive in-game bonuses and prizes. RID is not an investment product, and no return or price growth is promised.
